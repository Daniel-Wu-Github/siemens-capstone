Phase 2 predictive alert: four formulas

Example: available 35, min 20, max 90, history 8/day, schedule 10/day, lead 3 h

1 · Demand per day

demand = (history + schedule) / 2
If |schedule − history| / history > 50% → demand = schedule
|10 − 8| / 8 = 25% ≤ 50%
(8 + 10) / 2 = 9 / day

2 · Hours to minimum

hours = max(0, available − min) / demand × 24
(35 − 20) / 9 × 24 = 40 h

4 · Suggested quantity

qty = ceil(max − (available − demand × lead / 24))
90 − (35 − 9 × 3 / 24) = 56.125
ceil → 57 units
